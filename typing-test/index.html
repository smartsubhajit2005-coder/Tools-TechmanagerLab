How it works

WPM is correct characters typed divided by five, per minute. Accuracy is the share of keystrokes that matched the text. Errors count every wrong keystroke, even if you fix it afterward.

Example

300 correct characters in 1 minute is 60 WPM. If 15 of 330 keystrokes were wrong, accuracy is about 95%.

Limitations

Random common words do not include punctuation or capitals, so scores can differ from tests that do. Official exams may use other rules for counting errors. Check the rules of the test you are preparing for.

Privacy

Everything runs in your browser. Your typing is not sent anywhere or stored.

FAQ

What is a good typing speed?

Many office roles expect roughly 40 to 60 WPM at high accuracy. Check the requirement for your specific role or exam.

Can I pause the test?

Yes. Use Pause, then resume. Paused time is not counted.

Which keys can I use?

Esc restarts. Backspace corrects mistakes.
