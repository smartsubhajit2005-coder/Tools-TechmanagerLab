Word Counter

Count words, characters, sentences and reading time.

How to use

Paste or type text into the box. The counts update as you type.

Example

The text “The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog.” gives 9 words, 44 characters (36 without spaces) and 1 sentence.

Limitations

Words are groups of characters separated by spaces. Sentences are counted by the marks . ! and ?, so text without them shows 0 sentences. Reading time assumes 200 words per minute, which is an approximation. It counts pasted text only, not files.

Privacy

Input is processed by JavaScript on this page and is not sent anywhere by the tool. The page loads a font stylesheet from Google Fonts. See the privacy page.

FAQ

Is my text uploaded?

No. The count runs in JavaScript on this page and the text is not sent anywhere by the tool.

How is reading time calculated?

Word count divided by 200 words per minute, rounded to the nearest minute with a minimum of one minute. Real reading speed varies by person and text.
