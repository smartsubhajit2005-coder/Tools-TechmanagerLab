Frequently asked questions

What is net WPM in a typing test?

Net WPM = gross WPM minus (uncorrected errors ÷ minutes). Gross WPM counts all characters typed (1 word = 5 characters). Net WPM is the score most government exams use for pass/fail.

How many words are in the SSC typing test?

SSC CHSL/CGL LDC typing test: 35 net WPM in English (30 in Hindi), 10 minutes. The passage is roughly 1,750 characters minimum (about 350 words); the displayed passage may be longer so fast typists do not run out. SSC Stenographer: 80 WPM English / 100 WPM Hindi for 10 minutes of dictation.

What is KDPH in the Railway typing test?

KDPH = key depressions per hour. RRB requires 8,000 KDPH for English (about 30 WPM) and 7,000 KDPH for Hindi (about 25 WPM) in 10 minutes. Each key press counts, including spaces; backspace treatment varies by exam, so verify your notification.

How do I improve my typing speed for government exams?

Practice 10–15 minutes daily with correct finger placement (home row). Aim for accuracy first, then speed. Use this test in SSC/Railway/Court mode to match the real exam. Track your net WPM weekly and aim for steady, small gains.
