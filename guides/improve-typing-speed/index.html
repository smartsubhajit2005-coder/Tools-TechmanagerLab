How to improve typing speed without losing accuracy

Speed follows accuracy. If you correct many mistakes, slowing down slightly usually raises your real speed.

Measure first

Take the typing test once to set a baseline. Note WPM and accuracy. WPM here means correct characters divided by five, per minute. Use the same duration each time so results are comparable.

A simple routine

  1. Practice for 10 to 15 minutes, most days, rather than one long session.
  2. Keep your fingers on the home row and look at the screen, not the keys.
  3. Type at a pace where accuracy stays high, then raise the pace gradually.
  4. Retest weekly and compare accuracy as well as WPM.

Posture

Sit with relaxed shoulders, forearms roughly level and wrists neutral. Take short breaks. Stop if you feel pain.

What target should I aim for?

It depends on the role or exam. Check the requirement set by the employer or exam body, including how they count errors, because rules differ.
